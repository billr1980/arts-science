## User

How to make the shredding challenge go viral?

## Assistant

Make participation easier, more visual, and easier to share than simply visiting the website.

1. **Create a 20-second version**
   Show: choose a crime, say one rejection phrase, shred it, reveal the result. Add `#MagaShreddingChallenge` on-screen and end with “Make yours. Tag three people.”

2. **Give people a ready-made kit**
   Put printable labels, a one-page instruction sheet, caption suggestions, and a simple logo in one download folder. Make the first step obvious on `MagaShreddingChallenge.html`.

3. **Use a repeatable format**
   Every video should include:
   - The same opening shot or phrase
   - One specific printed crime
   - The shredding moment
   - A visible invitation to the next participant

4. **Seed it with a small group**
   Ask artists, organizers, comedians, local activists, and friends in different cities to post coordinated videos on the same day. Ten authentic posts are more useful than one polished announcement.

5. **Make the nomination loop explicit**
   Ask each participant to tag three people and name their city. Use a phrase such as: “I shredded this in Seattle. Who’s next?”

6. **Publish a living event map**
   Replace the placeholder `EVENTS.html` with a chronological gallery or city list. Feature new submissions quickly, with permission, so people can see that participation produces a visible result.

7. **Post natively everywhere**
   Upload separate versions to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Bluesky, and Facebook. Avoid relying only on links; put the call to action in the video and caption.

8. **Keep the tone human**
   Humor, anger, grief, and local specificity will travel better than a long political explanation. Invite people to write their own crime or phrase, while keeping the physical activity safe and symbolic.

9. **Track what spreads**
   Use distinct links or simple source tags for each partner and measure completed videos, event submissions, shares, and tagged participants, not just views.

The highest-leverage next build is a frictionless submission flow: downloadable kit, submission instructions, consent language, and a regularly updated events gallery. Viral reach can’t be guaranteed, but a strong participant-to-participant loop can be deliberately designed.
