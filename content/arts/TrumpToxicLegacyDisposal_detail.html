Trump Toxic Legacy Disposal

Trump Toxic Legacy Disposal is an ongoing series of public art interventions, premiering at the 2026 VENT! exhibition in Seattle.

"We spent the weekend feeding USAID into the wood chipper.", Elon Musk famously bragged when he condemned millions to famine, disease, and death.

The goal of this project is to have millions of Americans feed Trump's Toxic Legacy into the shredder - a means to purge our community of all traces of his poisonous reign.

Participants first pick a Trump Regime crime / atrocity to shred, or write their own.

They then feed it into the shredder while reciting words of disgusted rejection such as:
"I SHUN THIS THING"
"THIS IS REPULSIVE TO ME"
"I AM EXPELLING THIS POISON "
"I PURGE MYSELF OF THIS"
"I BANISH THIS EVIL"

The shredded paper will eventually be mixed with concrete and poured into a mold. The mold will stamp warnings onto the toxic block. The warnings will echo those designed for discouraging our future ancestors from disturbing long-term nuclear waste sites such as:
"NOTHING WE LOVE IS HERE"
"NOTHING OF VALUE IS HERE"
"THIS IS BEST SHUNNED AND UNTOUCHED"
"WHAT IS HERE IS DANGEROUS AND REPULSIVE TO US"
"EVIL IS ENTOMBED HERE"
"WHAT IS HERE HAS KILLED CHILDREN"

The other side will be marked with:
TRUMP TOXIC LEGACY ANTI-MEMORIAL

The toxic blocks will eventually by deposited in areas already rendered toxic by the Trump Regime, such as regional ICE headquarters, GEO Group for-profit concentration camps, and Mar el Largo, the Epicenter of Evil.
