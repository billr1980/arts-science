It's Jan 6 every day when you use a game controller to make a Trump dancing doll stomp on the U.S. Constitution—the harder he stomps, the more damage to America and the higher the score shown on the arcade display.

Trump’s backdrop shows some of his violent fascist cronies in the January 6 insurrection: the Proud Boys, Oath Keepers, 3 (III) Percenters, and Missouri Senator Josh Hawley. In keeping with Trump's carnival sideshow aesthetic, Dance Dance Insurrection is housed in a cheap cardboard enclosure emblazoned with garish stickers. And noisy?
