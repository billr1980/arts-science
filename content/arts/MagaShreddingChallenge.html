#MagaShreddingChallenge

Step 1: Get a Shredder

Find a paper shredder that you can use to turn printed pages into confetti-sized pieces. A small personal shredder is enough to get started.

Photo: Julo, Paper shredder, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.

Step 2: Print the Labels

Print out these shredder labels (the labels to use are in the filename). Or, just print on ordinary paper and tape it on.

ToxicLabels_Avery22890RectangleLabels.doc

Step 3: Festoon the shredder with the labels!

Step 4: Print out King Trump and his crimes.

Step 5: Cut the pages of crimes into slips and array on a table in front of the shredder.

Step 6: Get a camera or phone to record the shredding with (and a tripod if you have one).

Step 7: Invite your friends and neighbors for MAGA Shredding Party!

Send your video to bill@williamross.net and I'll add it to the EVENTS page!
