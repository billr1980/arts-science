#MagaShreddingChallenge

Choose one MAGA crime.
Say why you reject it.
Shred it.
Record it.
Invite someone else.
Repeat.

Before you begin

  1. Set up a paper shredder on a table.
  2. Print and attach the shredder labels from this kit.
  3. Print the crime pages from this kit, cut them into slips, and place them in front of the shredder.
  4. Set up a phone or camera so that the crime slip, your face, and the shredder labels are visible.

Make the video

  1. Pick a crime, or write your own.
  2. Read it aloud and reject it, saying something like: “I shun this thing,” “This is repulsive to me,” or “I purge myself of this.”
  3. Feed the paper into the shredder and let us know how you feel now after shredding.
  4. Post your video with #MagaShreddingChallenge and invite three people to make the next one.
  5. Send your video to bill@WilliamRoss.net and I'll post it to the EVENTS page!
